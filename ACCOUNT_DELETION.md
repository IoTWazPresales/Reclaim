## Reclaim – Account & Data Deletion

**Last updated:** 2025-02-18  

Reclaim is developed and operated by **Fission Corporation Pty Ltd**. This page explains how you can delete your personal data and effectively reset your account.

---

### Delete your data from inside the app

You can delete your data directly from the Reclaim app:

1. Open the **Reclaim** app.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Scroll to the **Data & privacy** section (or equivalent).
4. Tap **“Delete all data”**.
5. Confirm the prompt.

When you confirm:

- Your personal data stored by Reclaim (including medications, logs, mood history, sleep data, and mindfulness records) is **permanently removed** from our backend.
- You are **signed out** of the app.
- To use Reclaim again, you will need to sign in and start fresh.

> Note: This in-app delete action affects Reclaim’s copies of your data. It does **not** remove original data stored in external providers such as Google Health Connect or Apple HealthKit. You can revoke Reclaim’s access to those providers in your device’s settings.

---

### If you no longer have access to the app

If you cannot access the app (for example, you uninstalled it or changed devices), you can still request deletion of your data:

1. Send an email to **warren.eliason@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your Reclaim account.
2. Include a short note such as:  
   _“Please delete my Reclaim account and all associated personal data.”_

Once we verify your request, we will delete or anonymize your personal data from our systems, subject to any legal obligations to retain certain records. We aim to process deletion requests **within 30 days**.
